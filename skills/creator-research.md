Everything here serves seenjobs.io first: content that reaches job seekers, a brand creators WANT to cite, without Brandon begging anyone to promote it. Prepare only — never DM, follow, comment or post; never log in to Instagram; a CAPTCHA stops a search and is never solved.

1. Orient: `node scripts/act.mjs creators` — every account, its KEEP / PRUNE verdict with the reason, open unresolved knowledge, and why any account with evidence has 0 knowledge (with the fix, e.g. "resume instagram <user>").
2. Cross-creator evidence: `node scripts/act.mjs creator-insights` (add `type=hook` or `min=emerging` to narrow or widen). Quote the support exactly ("3 creators / 7 videos", strength, citation with timestamp). Say "emerging"/"anecdotal" out loud — never present weak lines as proven. `lastMeeting` says what the last board meeting was shown and which proposals cited it.
3. One creator: `node scripts/act.mjs creator <user>` — key points strongest first, each with this creator's own video + timestamp and which other creators back it.
4. Find new creators when Brandon asks who to look at, or when the library is thin: `node scripts/act.mjs discover <goal in plain words>` (Google in Brandon's Chrome via OpenCLI, about a minute). Two lists come back — LEARN from (content craft in the niche) and AUDIENCE fit (job-seeker audiences who would cite Seen's data), each with reasons read off the page and the Seen asset that fits (Weekly Ghost Report, Staffing Agency Ghost Index, employer grades, "Been ghosted?" page, listing signals, Résumé Studio, tracker). If OpenCLI is down it says so and returns the creators the researched ones mention plus the queries to run by hand — pass that on honestly.
5. Research the ones Brandon picks (or the top 1–3 audience fits when he asked you to choose): `node scripts/act.mjs queue-research <discovery id> <user…>` or `node scripts/act.mjs research <user> [count]`. Busy worker = queued, starts by itself. Check with `node scripts/act.mjs creator-status <user>`; report the real stage and error, never "done" before the job says complete.
6. Keep the tab lean: `node scripts/act.mjs prune plan` shows what would be archived and why; `node scripts/act.mjs prune` archives it (reversible with `restore-creators`). Tell Brandon the counts and the reasons. `purge-archived PURGE ARCHIVED` deletes archived folders for good — only when Brandon said to purge, in this conversation.
7. For an outreach idea, draft it as a proposal for Brandon (who, which Seen asset, the exact data point they could cite, the evidence line behind it) — never send anything.
